# Description

**Shafer’s sign (“tobacco dust”) = retinal tear until proven otherwise.** It appears as **tiny brown‑orange pigment granules** floating in the anterior vitreous, representing **RPE cells released through a retinal break**.

# Diagnostic Testing
-  Presentation / History Taking
	- PX reports **new floaters**, often “specks” or “dust”.
	- May report **flashes** or **peripheral shadow**.
	- Symptoms often **acute** and **unilateral**.
- Recommended Tests
	- Visual Acuity
	    - Assess baseline vision and detect any reduction from macular involvement.
	- **Biomicroscopy| Slit Lamp External Exam**
	    - Look specifically for **Shafer’s sign (tobacco dust)** in the anterior vitreous.
	    - Assess vitreous movement and clarity.
	    - Check for anterior segment cells/flare.
	    - Evaluate for posterior vitreous detachment spill.
	- **Fundoscopy| Dilated Fundus Exam**
	    - Identify **retinal tears**, **holes**, or **traction**.
		    - Look for **retinal detachment** or **vitreous hemorrhage**.
	    - Assess peripheral retina thoroughly.
	- **Pupil Testing**
	    - Rule out neurological causes of visual disturbance.
	- **Optical Coherence Tomography \| OCT**
	    - Evaluate for **retinal detachment**, macular involvement, or vitreoretinal traction.
	- **Visual Fields**
	    - Detect **field loss** suggestive of retinal detachment.
	- **Tonometry| IOP Assessment**
	    - Look for **low IOP** (possible detachment) or **high IOP** (secondary inflammation).
# Primary Associated Disease
- **Retinal Tear** Shafer’s sign strongly indicates **RPE pigment released through a retinal break**. It is **highly predictive** of a retinal tear in the presence of acute PVD symptoms.
# Differential Diagnosis
- **Posterior Vitreous Detachment (PVD)** If no Shafer’s sign and no tear is found; symptoms may still include flashes and floaters.
- **Retinal Detachment** If PX reports **curtain**, **field loss**, or progressive flashes.
- **Vitreous Hemorrhage** If traction from PVD or a tear causes a retinal vessel to rupture.
# Associated Symptoms
- New floaters (“dust”, “specks”, “pepper”)
- Flashes (photopsia)
- Cobweb‑like shadows
- Mild blur
- Possible peripheral field defect (if tear or detachment)
