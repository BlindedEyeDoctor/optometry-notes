# Description
---
A list of the clinical signs to look for when at the retina.
- Organized by mechanism of action. 
- Brief includes:
	- Affected structure
	- Mechanism of action
	- Appearance
	- Associated Disease

### Retinal Degenerations / Atrophy
- [[Cotton Wool Spot]]
	- Looks like Exudates
- [[Snail-track Degeneration]]
-  [[Atrophic Retinal Hole]]
	- Degeneration of a small portion of the retina
	- Not caused by traction
- [[Lattice Degeneration]]
	- Degeneration of Peripheral Retina. 
	- Myopia
		- Thinner retina due to enlongated eyeballs cause vascular issues
- [[RPE Degeneration]]
	- Loss of RPE due to failure to Choriod Failure to supply 
- [[Chororetinal Atrophy]]
	- Loss of Photoreceptors due to Choriod Failure to supply

### Retinal Structural Distortions
- [[Horseshoe Retinal Tear]]
- [[Linear Retinal Tear]]
- [[Operculated Retinal Hole]]
	- Operculum (bit of retina tissue) attached to retina
- [[Vitreoretinal Tuff]]
	- Strong Vitreous attachment in the periphery 

### Retinal Exudates / Deposits
- [[Exudates]]
	- "Hard Exudates, occlusion"
	- Hypereflective OCT 
	- Leakage of lipids
	- Defined boarder
	- Within Outer Plexiform Layer
	- Retinal vascular Occlusion
- [[Drusen]]
	- "The other one lol"
	- Hypereflective OCT
	- Leakage of lipids/proteins
	- Under RPE
	- Around Macula
	- Macula Degeneration
- [[Reticular Pseudo Drusen|RPD]]
	- Hypereflective OCT
- [[Embolism|Emboli]]
- [[Congenital Hypertrophy of the Retinal Pigment Epithelium|RPE Freckle]]
	- Deposition of melanin pigment at birth
	- Under Chroid 
- [[White Without Pressure]]
	- Peripheral Retina
	- A bit of tension in the retina leads to it appearing lighter
- [[White With Pressure]]
	- Putting a bit of pressure on the eyeball causes it to deform and streach, causing tension in the retina causing it to appear whiter

### Retinal Odema
- [[Diabetic Papillopathy]]
	- Optic nerve head
	- Swelling
	- Edges look less defined
	- Diabetes
- [[Optic Neuritis]]
	- Optic nerve head
	- Looks normal
	- Immune Disease
- [[Retinoschisis]]
	- Split the Outer plexiform Layer to the Inner Plexiform Layer
