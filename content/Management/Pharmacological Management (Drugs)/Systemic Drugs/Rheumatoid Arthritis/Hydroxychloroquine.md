#csDMARD 
#Drug 

- Used in #Monotherapy
- Low/Mild Disease
- #IDK_How_It_Works
- Severe #Ocular_Side_Effects
	- Dose Related: #Irreversible_Retinal_Damage
- Increase in duration of therapy
	- 1% per year up to 20% in 20 years.
	- "Bullseye appearance in retina"
		- Destruction of cones and rods
		- #Bilateral[[Macula Odema]] 
- ==🟡Eye exam base line 6-12 month.== 
- ==🟡Discontinue at the first sign of damage.==
- ==🟡Half life: 30-50 days.==
	- ==🟡Up to 250 days present in person and still causing damage.== 
	- ==🟡Can continue to worsen after taking the drug up to 1 year afterwards.== 
- Counsel patients so decisions can be made with their treatment.
	- Required adequate treatment
