Key Notes:
- Drug of Choice due to working much faster:
	- Shorter 6-12 Weeks.
	- See onset 3-6 weeks. 
	- Potentially be not on a corticosteroid as long. 
- Can be used with other csDMARDs.
	- If it doesn't have desired side effect. 
- ~80% will respond
- Low cost
- Decreases Atherosclerotic Heat Disease

Pharmacodynamics:
- [[Methotrexate]] is #Folic_Acid antagonist -> Inhibits #Dihydrofolate_Reductase -> Reduce #Purine and #Pyrimidines -> Inhibits DNA Synthesis
- Reduction in #IL-6, #TFN-Alpha
- Reduction in #T-Cell Proliferation, #Cytokine production, and #Reactive_Oxygen_Species
- Given with #Folic_Acid weekly to reduce side effects

Side Effects:
- #Dihydrofolate_Reductase required to convert #Folic_Acid to #Purine and #Pyrimidines 
- #Purine  and #Pyrimidines used in #DNA and #RNA synthesis. 
- No #DNA and #RNA -> Reduced proliferation of Neutrophils and cells in the GI tract and Liver. 
- Gastrointestinal: #GI_Ulceration
- Hepatic: #Hepatotoxicity
