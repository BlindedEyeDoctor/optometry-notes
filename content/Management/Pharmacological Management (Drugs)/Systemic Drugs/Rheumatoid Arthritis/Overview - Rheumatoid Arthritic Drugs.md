# Rheumatoid Arthritic Drugs
---
Patients who have [[Rheumatoid Arthritis]] are prescribed Disease-Modifying Anti-Rheumatic Drugs (DMARDs). 
#Management = #Pharmacological_Mangement 
#Systemic_Drugs  = #DMARDs
# Key Notes
---
- Rheumatoid Arthritic = RA
- RA is treated by Disease-Modifying Anti-Rheumatic Drugs = [[Overview - Rheumatoid Arthritic Drugs|DMARDs]]
	- Targets Inflammatory Markers: Modulate Cytokines
- ==🟡Conventional Synthetics DMARDs==: [[csDMARD]]
- ==🟡Biological DMARDs==: [[bDMARD]]
	- Monoclonal Antibodies:
		- TFN-Alpha Antagonist
		- IL-1 Receptor Agonist
		- IL-6 Receptor Agonist
- ==🟡Targeted Synthetic DMARDs==: [[tsDMARD]]
		- Targeting other processes
- Conventional DMARD first -> then bDMARD or csDMARD
- Symptom Management
	- [[NSAIDs]]
	- [[CCS]]
		- Can be used in RA because of anti-inflammatory and immunosuppressant effects. 
	- Used individually or together to treat the acute flares.
- Significant Toxicity

# Trivia - DMARDs
---
-> Typically as patients are being introduced to DMARDs, they would be on pain management. Chances are that if a patient has had RA for multiple years, they would be on a DMARD or two. 
- Rarely goes into Drug free remission. 
- Hard to get patients back onto the drug 
- Patients on treatment indefinitely
