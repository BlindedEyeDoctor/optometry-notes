# Effect of Aldosterone 
---
**AT1 Receptors** 
- @ #Heart, #Arteries, #Kidney, #Adrenal_Cortex, #Lungs, and #Brain
- ==Vasoconstriction==
	- #AT2 @ #Arteries -> #Vasoconstriction
- ==Hypertension==
	- #AT2 @ #Adrenal_Cortex -> #Aldosterone 
	- #Aldosterone@ #Distal_Convoluted_Tubule -> Increased Sodium Retention -> #Hypertention
