	- #Hyperkalemia
		- Aldosterone = Sodium Water Retension
		- No Aldosterone = Loose Sodium and Water
		- Increased Potassium (K+) levels

		- AT2 = Powerful Vasoconstrictor, also constricts #Efferent_Arteriolee, exit pipe of kidneys.
		- No AT2 = Less Vasoconstriction, more water outflow = Less filtration or #Renal_Impairment
