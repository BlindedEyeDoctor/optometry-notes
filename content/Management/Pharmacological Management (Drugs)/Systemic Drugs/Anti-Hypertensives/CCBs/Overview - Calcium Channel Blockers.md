# Calcium Channel Blockers
---
- Calcium Channel Blockers (CCB)
- Inhibition of ==L-Type Voltage-Gated Sodium Channels==
- [[Dihydropyridine Calcium Channel Blockers]]
	- Mainly ==Vasodilation== @ Peripheral Arteries
- [[Non-Dihydropyridine Calcium Channel Blockers]]
	- Cardiac Dysrhythmia 
	- Angina (Chest pain due to lack of blood).
	- ==Decreased Heart Rate==

# CCB - Effects
---
- ==Smooth muscles== have **Long-lasting-Type (L-Type), Voltage-gated Calcium Channels**.
	- #L-Type_Calcium_Channels are commonly located on ==vascular smooth muscle cells and cardiac myocytes== of the #Heart & #Arteries 
	- But also the #Intestines of the #GI-Tract 
- Inhibition stops the flow of calcium (Ca2+) into  smooth muscle.
- This causes #Muscle_Relaxation in #Smooth_Muscles, causing:
- #Vasodialation in #Arteries 
- #Bradycardia in the #Heart 
- #Constapation  in the #GI-Tract
