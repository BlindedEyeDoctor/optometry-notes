# Diuretics
---
- Block sodium and/or chloride reabsorption in the nephron, ==Hypokalemia==.
- Water goes with the solution with higher osmotic pressure,  ==Polyuria==. 
- Water is not reabsorbed into the blood. 
- Reduce blood volume, ==Hypotension==.
- [[Loop Diuretics]], [[Thiazides]], [[Potassium-sparing Diuretics]], & [[Carbonic Anhydrase Inhibitors]].

# Diuretics Effects
---
==Hypotension==
- #Hypovolemia -> #Hypotension 
	- Reduction in blood volume
	- #Dizzyness #Headaches #Lightheaded
==Hypokalemia==
- #Hypokalemia  -> #Electrolyte_Imbalances
	- Decrease electrolyte levels due to [[Thiazides]] impacting on Potassium.
- #Acid-Base_Imbalances
	- Increase PH due to removal of H+ ions in [[Potassium-sparing Diuretics]].
- #Alkali_Urine 
	- Due to increased bicarbonate concentration in urine in [[Carbonic Anhydrase Inhibitors]].
==Polyuria==
- #Polyuria -> #Dehydration 
	- Peeing more, due to Mechanism of Action
