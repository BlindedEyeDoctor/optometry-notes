# Beta Blockers Effects
---
- Beta Blockers bind to ==Beta Adrenoreceptors== and serve as a competitive antagonist to #Catecholamines preventing their binding to Beta-1 and Beta-2 Receptors. 
- Blocking #Beta-1_Receptor 
	- Decreased cardiac workload and vascular resistance
	- #Bradycardia& #Vasodialation  
- Blocking #Beta-2_Receptor
	-  #Brochospasm & #Bronchoconstriction
