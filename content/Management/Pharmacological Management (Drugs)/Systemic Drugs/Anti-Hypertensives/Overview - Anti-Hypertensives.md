# Key Notes
---
- ==Hypertension = High blood pressure==
	- Factors:
		- Blood Volume 
		- Vessel Length and Radius 
		- Heart Rate 
	- Three ways to deal with it:
		- ==Hypovolemia==
			- Less blood, less pressure -> Diuretics (Pee more)
		- ==Vasodilation ==
			- Wider vessels, more space for blood -> less resistance  
		- ==Brachycardia==
			- Less pressure
- Diuretics
	- ==Loop Diuretics==
		- Strongest Diuretic
	- ==Potassium Sparing Diuretics==
		- Hyperkalemia
	- ==Thiazides==
		- Hypokalemia
	- ==Carbonic Anhydrase Inhibitors==
		- Alkali Urine
- ==AT2 Blocker==
	- Angiotensin 2 -> Vasoconstrictor 
	- AT1 Receptor -> Heart, kidneys, adrenal cortex, lungs, and brain.
	- AT2 Receptor ->  Fetus Development 
- ==ACE Inhibitor==
	- Blocks ACE, which allows: AT1 -> AT2
- Calcium Channel Blockers
	- ==Dihydropyridine CCB==
		- Smooth Muscle Relaxation:
		- Arteries, GI-Tract
	- ==Non-Dihydropyridine CCB==
		- Heart
- ==Beta Blocker==
	- Beta 1 -> Heart
		- ==Beta-1 Selective==
	- Beta 2 -> Lungs
		- ==Non-selective Beta Blocker==
