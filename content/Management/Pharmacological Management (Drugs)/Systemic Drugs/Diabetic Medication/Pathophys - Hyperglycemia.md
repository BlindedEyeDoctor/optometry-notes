- Insulin is released from the gut in response to food intake. 
	- It stimulates glucose-dependent insulin release
	- Slow gut emptying (GLP-1)
	- Suppresses apatite (GLP-1)
	- Inhibits Post-prandial release of glucacgon (GLP-1)
	- Increase glucagon secretion (GIP)
	- enhances uptake of glucose (TGs) into adipose (GIP)
- It also is inactivated by DPP-4
