#First_Line for #Type_2_Diabetes, #Diabetes 
- Key info:
	- Renally Cleared
		- Dose adjusted if renal function is reduced
		- Extra care if Patient has condition that may affect renal function or cause tissue hypoxia or acidosis. 
- Mechanism of Action:
	- Reduce Glucose Absorption from the GIT
	- Decreases Hepatic Glucose Production
	- Increase Perpheral use of glucose
		- Increase Glucose up take into cells
		- Improves glucose sensitivity of tissues
- Adverse Effects:
	- #GI_Disorders 
	- Malsbsorption of the Vitamin B12
	- #Lactic_Acidosis
	- Acute Hepatitis
