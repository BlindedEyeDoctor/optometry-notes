# Description 
---
- Two types of Anxiety
	- Normal state that helps you focus your attention. 
	- Pathologic when it is out of proportion to the stimulus
- Can manifest symptoms relating to the autonomic nervous system
	- E.g., tachycardia, dry mouth, insomnia, anorexia, diarrhoea, dyspnoea, etc
- Have a variety of types and subsequent treatment methodologies.
- Have a variety of symptoms
	- GAD, panic attacks & panic disorder, social anxiety disorder, OCD, body dysmorphic disorder
