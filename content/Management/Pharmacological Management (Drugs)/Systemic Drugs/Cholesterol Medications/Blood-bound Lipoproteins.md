# Physiology of Blood-Bound Lipoproteins
---
![[Image - Lipoproteins Diagram.png]]

Four-types of blood bound lipoproteins.
1. ***High-density lipoporteins*** #HDL
	- Transports excess #Cholesterol  back to #Liver.
2. Intermediate-density lipoproteins #IDL
	- Transport #Cholesterol  and #Triglycerides  throughout the body
3. ***Low-density lipoproteins*** #LDL
	- Transports #Cholesterol throughout the body
4. Very-low-density lipoproteins #VLDL
	- Transports #Triglycerides to #Adapose_Tissue and #Muscle_Tissue
