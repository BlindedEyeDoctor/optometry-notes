# Key Notes
---
- Dyslipidemia -> High concentration of [[Blood-bound Lipoproteins]]
	- Derivation: 
		- In Vivo Production
			- Main source, From Liver
		- From Diet 
			- Through absorption in GI tract
	- Storage:
		- #LDL binds to #LDL-Receptors on surface of cells, storing Cholesterol.
	- Elimination/Processing:
		- #HDL transports excess Cholesterol to the liver to be broken down or flushed out as waste via Bile.
	- Five methods to deal with it 
		- Reduce cholesterol synthesis @ Liver
		- Increase cholesterol transport to Liver 
			- for Elimination/Processing
		- Prevent dietary cholesterol from diffusing @ Gut Lumen
		- Prevent cholesterol re-absorption @ Gut Lumen
		- Improve bile acid elimination
	- Note: you may also have to get cholesterol out from the cells of your body.
