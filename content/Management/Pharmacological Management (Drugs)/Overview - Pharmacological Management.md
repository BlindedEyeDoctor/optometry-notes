# Organization
---
As [[Overview - Pharmacological Management|pharmacological management]] will primarily be focused on drugs, notation will be organized as follows:
1. Pages will first be broken down into #Ocular_Drugs or #Systemic_Drugs based on the [[Overview - Pharmacological Management#Drug Dosing Methods|method of administration]].
2. Pages will then be broken down into the specific #Systemic_Condition or otherwise linked to a #Ocular_Disease. 
 3. Pages will furthermore be assigned a #Drug_Catagory based on the drug's mechanism of action. Note: one or more drug category may be assigned to treat a disease. 
4.  Following that, it will be categorized into specific #Drug_Class based on specific receptor binding. 
5.  And then finally into #Drug to show the actual constituents/active ingredients, receptor specificity etc.

# Drug Grades
---
In Adelaide there are a total of 10 schedules for drugs in South Australia, however Optometrists only need to know 5. These are: 
- Schedule 1
	- Off the counter
- Schedule 2
	- Pharmacy
- Schedule 3
	- Behind the Counter
- Schedule 4
	- Prescription
- Schedule 8
	- Prescription Controlled

# Drug Dosing Methods
---
There are many ways to administer a drug. From MMED3934 - Principles of Pharmacology and Toxicology - we learned about the following methods of delivery:
- Oral 
	- GI tract Absorption
- Intravenous 
	- Venous Injection
- Intraarterial
	- Arterial injection
- Intramuscular
	- Muscular injection
- Gels/Patches
	- Epidermis Absorption
- Topical Ophthalmic Administration
	- Eye drops

In OPTO 3010 - Ocular pharmacology - we additionally learned the following methods of delivery:
- Ocular Inserts
	- Intravitreal Implants to dose drug at a steady pace to aid compliance.

- Intracameral
	- [Anterior Chamber] Injection
- Periocular: Subconjunctival, Sub-tenon, Peribulbar, Retrobulbar
	- Bypass [Conjunctiva] and [Corneal Epithelium]
- Intraocular/Intravitreal
	- [Vitreous Humor](obsidian://open?vault=Diseases%20and%20Associated%20Symptoms&file=Optometry%20Notes%2FManagement%2FDrugs%2FVitreous%20Humor) Injection
