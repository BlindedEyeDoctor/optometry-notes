# Description
---
Cataract is an opacity of the crystalline lens (or its capsule) that may cause a visual disability as perceived by the patient due to light scatter, aberration and/or absorption of light.

# Grading
--- 
- Lens Opacities Classification System (LOCS) III Grading
	- Uses standard photographs to grade nuclear, cortical and posterior subcapsular cataracts
	- Standards regularly spaced along the continuum of cataract
	- Decimal scale: grader decides which interval the known image falls with a decimal grade
	- Done via ==DILATED PUPIL==
	- Example: NO 3.7 NC 3.2 C1.9 P 0.1 (Decimal grade assigned from standard photographs at integer boundaries)
	• NO = Nuclear opalescence/haze, scale [0-6]
	• NC= Nuclear Brunescence, scale [0-6]
	• C = Cortical, scale [0-5]
	• P = Posterior, scale [0-5]
