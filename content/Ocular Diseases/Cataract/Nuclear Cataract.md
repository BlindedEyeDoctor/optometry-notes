# Description
---
Opacification and **Brunescence** (yellowing) of the **Central Lens Nucleus**. Progressive **Myopic Shift** causing "**Second Sight**” temporarily letting Presbyopic PX to read without glasses. Most common age‑related cataract.

# TLDR
---
- Central Vision
- Lens Nucleus 
- Brunescence
- Myopic Shift
- Increased Refractive Index
- Reduced Red Reflex
- Cloudy Appearance
# Linked Symptoms
---
- Blurred Vision,  Contrast Sensitivity Loss|Reduced Contrast Sensitivity
	- Light scatter decreases the amount of light falling correctly on the retina.
- Glare|Glare Sensitivity
	- Light Scatter causes mild glare
- Myopia|Myopic Shift
	- Due to the increased refractive index caused by protein aggregation and high molecular weight compound formation within lens crystallin.
- Color Vision Loss
	- Brunescence: yellow/brown hue
	- Blue light blocked by yellowing of lens fibres 
	- Caused by the additive post-translational modifications ie. tryptophan leading to the formation of fluorescent chromophores

# Risk Factors
---
- Age
	- Decline in Antioxidants and Compaction
- UV Exposure
# Pathophysiology
--- 
Age-related nuclear cataracts are caused by a combination of the aforementioned mechanisms of
cataract formation, which occur with age. Environmental factors such as UV light, toxins, drugs, and
systemic diseases (i.e. diabetes) can also play a role in triggering these mechanisms.
1. Altered enzyme, growth factor and other protein levels lead to changes in gene expression
2. Changes in sodium and calcium ion transport leading to osmotic imbalances and the formation of intracellular vacuoles and high-molecular-weight aggregates
3. Activation of calpains causing disruption of normal crystallin structure and light refraction
4. Post-translational modification of crystallin's causing conformational changes and changing their refractive properties ie. glycation or modification of tryptophan causing fluorescent chromophores and Brunescence
5. Oxidation of lens proteins (another form of PTM)
6. Loss of defense mechanisms – decreased antioxidants and formation of cortex-nuclear barrier
