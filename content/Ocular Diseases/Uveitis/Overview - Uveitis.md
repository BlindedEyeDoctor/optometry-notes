# Uveitis
---
[[Overview - Uveitis|Uveitis]], in essence is the inflammation of the [[Uvea]], but it also encompasses several different layers that are in close proximity.
#Ocular_Diseases = #Uveitis
# Forms - Types
---
Uveitis comes in four forms. 
- Choroiditis
	- Inflammation of the [[Choroid]]
- Vitritis
	- Inflammation of the [[Vitreous Humor]]
- Retinitis
	- Inflammation of the [[Retina]]
- Neuro-retinitis 
	- Inflammation of the [[Optic Nerve]]
