# Cause:
- Bacterial infection within eyelid gland (Zeis, Moll or meibomian)
# Signs/symptoms:
- Well-defined, hyperaemic subcutaneous nodule, with surrounding swelling
- Tender to touch and may cause a generalised ache or pain on blinking
