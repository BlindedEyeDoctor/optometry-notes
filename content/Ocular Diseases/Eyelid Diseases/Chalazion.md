# Cause:
- Granulomatous inflammatory lesion caused by retained sebaceous secretion
# Signs/symptoms:
- Well-defined, enlarging nodule located in the tarsal plate
- May be inflamed initially, appearing similar to hordeloa with erythema, oedema and tenderness
- Chronic lesions are firm, flesh coloured and painless.
