- Angiotensin 2 causes hypertension. 
	- We want to inhibit it. 
- AT1 Receptors
	- Found in the heart, blood vessels, kidney, adrenal cortex, lungs and brain. 
	- Vasoconstriction
	- ==Angiotensin 2 binds to AT1 releasing aldosterone. This leads to increased sodium retention at the distal convoluted tubule. Due to the increased osmotic pressure, more water is reabsorbed into the blood thus increasing blood pressure.==
- AT2 Receptors
	- Found in the adult myometrium, and in the fetus and neotate.
	- ==regulates apoptosis, vascular growth, cellular differentiation, fetal development, and neuronal regeneration.==
