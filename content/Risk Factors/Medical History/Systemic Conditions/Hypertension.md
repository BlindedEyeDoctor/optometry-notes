# Hypertension
---
Hypertension = High blood pressure
#RiskFactors #Systemic_Condition #Blood 
# Method of action
---
- Increased Blood Output
	- High Blood Volume
- Increased Peripheral Vascular Resistance
	- Low Constriction
- Faster Heart Rate
	- Faster Diastole Rate
# Causes
--- 
- Primary - Develops over time
	- Genetics
	- Smoking
	- Obecity
	- High Sodium
	- High Alcohol Consumption
	- Stress
	- Lack of Exercise
	- Aging
- Secondary - Occurs quickly and more severely
	- Obstructive sleep apnea
	- Heart defects
	- Kidney disease
	- Medications or illegal drugs
	- Adrenal or thyroid conditions
