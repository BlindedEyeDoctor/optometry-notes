# Description
---
Hyperglycemia is simply put a higher than average blood sugar concentration in the blood which is the harmful mechanism of Diabetes.
# Types
---
There are three types of diabetes. 
1. Type 1 Diabetes
	- Insulin is not produced
	- Typically Genetic
2. Type 2 Diabetes
	- Insulin resistance
	- Genetically Disposed to Asian
3. Gestational diabetes
	- Diabetes that occurs during pregnancy. 

Presents as: fatigue, polydipsia/polyuria, increased hunger, weight loss, poor wound healing, frequent opportunistic infections, blurred vision. 
# Insulin
---
Insulin is the hormone produced by beta cells of the pancreas which regulates energy conservation and storage in the body. 
It stimulates cellular uptake of glucose and amino acids in the periphery. 
Promotes synthesis of complex molecules
- glucose to glycogen in the liver
- Amino acids to proteins in the muscles
- fatty acids to triglycerides in the adipose tissues 
Acts by inhibiting glucose production and release in the liver, inhibiting proteolysis and lipolysis. 

Primary stimulus for insulin release is in an increase in blood glucose levels. 
- Other stimuli include:
- Incretin hormones
	- GLP1
	- GIP
- Sympathetic nervous system stimulation
	- Beta-2 receptor activation
