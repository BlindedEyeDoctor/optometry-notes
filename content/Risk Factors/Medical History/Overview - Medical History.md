# Description
---
Medical history captures the patient’s broader health context including the systemic diseases, chronic conditions, and physiological patterns that influence how the eye behaves and responds to stress. These factors don’t act on the eye directly, but they shape baseline vulnerability, modify disease expression, and guide clinical decision‑making. A well‑documented medical history is essential for interpreting ocular findings accurately and predicting risk across multiple disease pathways.
