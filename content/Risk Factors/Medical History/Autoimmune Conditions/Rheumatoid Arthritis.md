# Rheumatoid Arthritis 
---
- Rheumatoid Arthritis (RA)
- ~2% of Australian Population 
	- Initiate therapy as early as possible 
- 1 in 5 patients experience [[Overview - Rheumatoid Arthritic Drugs#Related Ocular Context|ocular manifestations]] of the disease. 
- Multi-organ system affecting diseases. 
	- #Joints (Rheumatoid Nodules development)
	- #Cardiovascular (Extra articular - Rheumatoid Vasculitis)
		- If Cardiovascular Manifestations = Not having Adequate Treatment of Rheumatoid Arthritis 
- #Autoimmune_Conditions  
- [[Overview - Rheumatoid Arthritic Drugs|Drug therapy]] aims to minimize functional impairments of patients
	- Typically 10 years after diagnosis 
		- <50% of patients will loose the ability to work full time due to functional impairment
		- ~80% of patients would have miss-aligned joints not to the point where they can't work

# Pathophysiology - RA
---
Influx of different immune cells into the Synovium (the membrane surrounding/protecting the cartilage) of the joints.
- Destruction of the joints
	- Erosion that happens long term. 
	- Changes to shape and mobility of the joints as we loose cartilage. 
- Blood vessel formation
	- Inflammation - Due to additional [[Overview - Neovascularization#Pathophysiology - Angiogenesis|angiogenesis]] that occurs.
Manifestations:
- Most commonly on fingers
	- Both sides of the body.
- Extra-articular manifestations
	- Eyes, cardiovascular system, nodules
- Flare ups:
	- Inflammation will ebb and flow over time
	- Acutely painful flareups that develop functional impairment. 
- Long term:
	- Chronic erosion occurs. 
	- Can get functional impairment that is associated with chronic and life long. 
-> Avoid chronic symptoms with drug therapy

# Pathogenesis of RA
---
- Auto-immune joint destruction
- Drugs that target different places. 
- 
	- CD20 Receptor
		- Anti-CD20 Drug, Monoclonal Antibody - Rituximab, To stop the B cell activation
- Cytokines that are released by macrophages that are upregulated in RA:
	- Interleukin 1 (IL1)
		- IL1 Inhibitor/Antagonist
			- Anakinra
	- Interleukin 6 (IL6)
		- IL6 Inhibitor/Antagonist
			- Tocilizumab, Sarilumab
	- Tumor Necrosis Factor (TFN) 
		- TFN Inhibitor/Antagonist
			-  Etanercept, adalimumab, infliximab, golimumab
- Inflammation 
	- NSAIDS
		- Meloxicam
		- Naproxen Sodium Ibuprofen 
	- Corticosteroids
		- Prednisone
