# Risk Factors
---
#Risk_Factors  
Risk factors are characteristics or exposures that increase a patient’s likelihood of developing ocular disease. They don’t cause the disease directly, but they shift the patient’s baseline vulnerability making certain pathologies more probable, more severe, or more rapidly progressive.
