**Pattern:** Deep red‑purple hue; may be diffuse or nodular. **Vessels:** Do **not** blanch with phenylephrine; severe pain.

**Common causes:**

- Autoimmune disease (RA, GPA, lupus)
    
- Infectious (rare)
    

**Key clues:**

- **Severe, boring pain radiating to temple/jaw**
    
- **Pain on eye movement**
    
- **Vision threat**
