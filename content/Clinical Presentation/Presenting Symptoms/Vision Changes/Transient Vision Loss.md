# Associated Diseases

**Takeaway:** Transient = **vascular spasm**, **migraine**, or **ICP‑related**.

### 🔥 Vascular / Ischemic

- **Amaurosis fugax** (carotid disease → precursor to BRAO/CRAO)
    
- **Intermittent retinal ischemia**
    
- **Intermittent angle closure** (IOP spikes)
    

### 🔥 Neurological

- **Migraine aura** Scintillating scotoma, zigzags, shimmering field loss.
    
- **Occipital lobe ischemia / TIA**
    

### 🔥 Intracranial Pressure

- **Papilledema** Transient visual obscurations (seconds‑long dimming).
    

### 🔥 Optic Nerve

- **Optic neuritis (early)** Brief episodes of dimming before full onset.
    

### 🔥 Systemic

- **Orthostatic hypotension**
    
- **Cardiac arrhythmias causing brief hypoperfusion**
