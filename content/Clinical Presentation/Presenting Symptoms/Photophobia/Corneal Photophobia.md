Photophobia here is due to **exposed corneal nerves**.

- **Corneal Abrasion** Sharp pain + photophobia + tearing.
    
- **Corneal Foreign Body** Photophobia + pain on blinking.
    
- **Keratitis (all types)**
    
    - **Bacterial keratitis**
        
    - **Viral keratitis (HSV, VZV)**
        
    - **Fungal keratitis**
        
    - **Acanthamoeba keratitis** Severe photophobia, often out of proportion to signs.
        
- **Recurrent Corneal Erosion** Morning pain + photophobia.
    
- **Contact Lens Overwear / Hypoxia** Photophobia + discomfort + SPK.
    

**Clinical pearl:** Photophobia + pain + epithelial defect → **cornea**.
