Photophobia here is due to **iris sphincter spasm**.

- **Anterior Uveitis (Iritis)** Consensual photophobia is classic.
    
- **Intermediate Uveitis** Less common but still photophobic.
    
- **Posterior Uveitis (rare photophobia)** Usually mild unless anterior chamber involved.
    

**Clinical pearl:** Photophobia + circumlimbal flush → **uveitis**.
