Superficial eyelid swelling **anterior to the orbital septum**.

### **Associated Diseases**

- **Preseptal Cellulitis**
    
- **Chalazion / Hordeolum (Stye)**
    
- **Allergic eyelid swelling**
    
- **Blepharitis**
    

### **Associated Symptoms**

- Tender eyelid
    
- Warmth
    
- Redness
    
- **NO pain with eye movement**
    
- **NO proptosis**
    
- Vision normal
