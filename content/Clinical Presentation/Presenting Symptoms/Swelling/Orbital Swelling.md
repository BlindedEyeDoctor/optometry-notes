Deep swelling **posterior to the septum** — dangerous.

### **Associated Diseases**

- **Orbital Cellulitis**
    
- **Idiopathic Orbital Inflammation (IOI)**
    
- **Thyroid Eye Disease (TED)**
    
- **Orbital Tumours / Masses**
    

### **Associated Symptoms**

- **Pain with eye movement**
    
- **Proptosis**
    
- **Reduced motility**
    
- Diplopia
    
- Fever (cellulitis)
    
- Vision changes
    
- RAPD (severe cases)
