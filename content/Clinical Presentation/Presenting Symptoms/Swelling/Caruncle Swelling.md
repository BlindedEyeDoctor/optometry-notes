**What it is:** Inflammation of the caruncle (medial canthus fleshy tissue).

**Associated diseases:**

- Carunculitis
    
- Viral conjunctivitis
    
- Allergic conjunctivitis
    
- Canaliculitis (adjacent)
    

**Associated symptoms:**

- Medial canthus redness
    
- Tenderness
    
- Mucous discharge
    
- Epiphora
