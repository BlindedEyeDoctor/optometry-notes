Swelling in the **superotemporal orbit** — the lacrimal gland.

### **Associated Diseases**

- **Dacryoadenitis** (infectious or inflammatory)
    
- **Sarcoidosis**
    
- **Sjogren’s syndrome**
    
- **Lacrimal gland tumours**
    

### **Associated Symptoms**

- **S‑shaped ptosis**
    
- Pain in superotemporal orbit
    
- Swelling of outer upper lid
    
- Diplopia (rare)
    
- Fever (if infectious)
