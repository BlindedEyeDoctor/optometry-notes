Raised, **flat‑topped**, vascular swellings on tarsal conjunctiva.

### **Associated Diseases**

- **Allergic Conjunctivitis**
    
- **Vernal Keratoconjunctivitis (VKC)**
    
- **Atopic Keratoconjunctivitis (AKC)**
    
- **Bacterial Conjunctivitis**
    
- **Contact Lens Papillary Conjunctivitis (GPC)**
    

### **Associated Symptoms**

- **Itching**
    
- Mucous discharge
    
- Redness
    
- Contact lens intolerance
    
- Grittiness
    
- Photophobia (if severe)
