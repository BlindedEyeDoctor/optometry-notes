These are the _classic_ sources of throbbing pain.

### **Associated Diseases**

- **Migraine** Unilateral throbbing pain + photophobia + aura.
    
- **Cluster Headache** Severe orbital throbbing + tearing + ptosis.
    
- **Temporal Arteritis (GCA)** Throbbing temporal pain + jaw claudication + vision risk.
    
- **Sinusitis (frontal or ethmoid)** Throbbing pressure behind eyes + congestion.
    
- **Acute Angle‑Closure Glaucoma** Severe throbbing pain + halos + nausea.
    
- **Intermittent Angle Closure** Episodic throbbing pain + blur + halos.
    
- **Uveitic Glaucoma** Throbbing pain + photophobia + redness.
- 
- **Anterior Uveitis** Deep ache + photophobia + circumlimbal flush.
    
- **Scleritis (less common)** Usually boring pain, but can feel throbbing in vascular subtypes.
    
- **Severe Conjunctivitis (rare)** Vascular engorgement → throbbing discomfort.
- 
- **Orbital Cellulitis** Throbbing pain + fever + pain with eye movement.
    
- **Thyroid Eye Disease (TED)** Throbbing pressure + lid retraction + diplopia.
    
- **Idiopathic Orbital Inflammation (IOI)** Throbbing pain + swollen lids + painful ophthalmoplegia.
