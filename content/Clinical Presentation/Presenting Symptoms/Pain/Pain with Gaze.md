This is a _deep, movement‑triggered pain_ — not surface irritation.

### **Associated Diseases**

- **Optic Neuritis (Retrobulbar)** Pain on eye movement + reduced colour vision + decreased acuity. Often described as “it hurts when I look up or to the side.”
    
- **Orbital Cellulitis** Severe pain with eye movement + fever + proptosis + reduced motility. A medical emergency.
    
- **Thyroid Eye Disease (TED)** Pain or pressure with gaze, especially upgaze; tight, fibrotic EOMs. Often accompanied by lid retraction and proptosis.
    
- **Idiopathic Orbital Inflammation (Orbital Pseudotumor)** Painful ophthalmoplegia + acute onset + swollen lids. Pain increases with movement in the direction of the inflamed muscle.
    
- **Scleritis (Posterior)** Deep boring pain that worsens with eye movement. Often misdiagnosed unless you look for systemic autoimmune clues.
    
- **Sinus‑related Orbital Apex Syndromes** Pain with gaze + cranial nerve involvement. Usually associated with sinus disease or infection.
    

### **Clinical pearl:**

Pain with gaze direction → **think orbit or optic nerve**, not cornea or conjunctiva.
