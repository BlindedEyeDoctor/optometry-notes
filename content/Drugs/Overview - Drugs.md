# Description
--- 
Drugs are substances that causes a change in an organism's physiology. In healthcare we use drugs to prevent or to treat disease. 
# Drug Grades
---
In Adelaide there are a total of 10 schedules for drugs in South Australia, however Optometrists only need to know 5. These are: 
- Schedule 1
	- Off the counter
- Schedule 2
	- Pharmacy
- Schedule 3
	- Behind the Counter
- Schedule 4
	- Prescription
- Schedule 8
	- Prescription Controlled
