# Description
--- 
- 1 in 5 Australians have experienced a mental health disorder in the previous 12 months
- Most of these people filled a mental health-related prescription during this time
- Anxiety disorders are the most common type of mental health disorder, depressive disorders are #2
- Many of the drugs to manage depression and/or anxiety are in the top 50 most dispensed in Australia
