# Description
---
Patients who have [Hypertension|high blood pressure] are prescribed medication to reduce it. 
# @ a Glance
---
- Hypertension = High blood pressure
- Factors:
	- Blood Volume 
	- Vessel Length and Radius 
	- Heart Rate 
- Two ways to deal with it:
	- Decrease Blood Volume
		- Less blood, less pressure -> Diuretics (Pee more)
	- Vasodilation 
		- Wider vessels, more space for blood -> less resistance  
- Angiotensin 2 - Vasoconstrictor 
- AT1 @ Heart, kidneys, adrenal cortex, lungs, and brain.
