# Description
---
Superior Oblique Muscle
# Interesting Facts
---
- Only cranial nerve to exit dorsally from the brainstem
- Thinnest and longest intracranial nerve -> easily damaged
- Enters orbit outside the Annulus of Zinn (CN4 does NOT pass through the annulus)
# Pathway
---
- T**rochlear Nerve**
- Brainstem – Posterior Midbrain
- Subarachnoid Space
	- Wraps around the brainstem laterally
- Cavernous Sinus
	- Runs in the lateral wall (superior to CN III, inferior to CN V1)
- Superior Orbital Fissure
- **Superior Oblique Muscle**
	- Function: **Depression in adduction, Intorsion, Abduction component**
