# Description
---
Facial Sensation + Mastication
# Interesting Facts
---
- Largest Cranial Nerve
- Corneal Reflex (Touch Cornea = Blink)
# Pathway
---
1. **Trigeminal Nerve**
2. Pons (sensory + motor roots)
3. Meckel’s Cave
4. **Trigeminal (Gasserian) Ganglion**
5. **Three Divisions:**
	- **V1 – Ophthalmic** → Superior Orbital Fissure
	- **V2 – Maxillary** → Foramen Rotundum
	- **V3 – Mandibular** → Foramen Ovale
6. **Peripheral Branches:**
	- **V1**: Frontal, Lacrimal, Nasociliary
	- **V2**: Infraorbital, Zygomatic, Superior alveolar
	- **V3**: Buccal, Auriculotemporal, Lingual, Inferior alveolar
7. **Motor Branches (V3):**
	- Masseter
	- Temporalis
	- Medial & Lateral Pterygoids
	- Mylohyoid
	- Anterior Digastric
	- Tensor Tympani
	- Tensor Veli Palatini
