# Pathway - Venous Drainage
---
1. **Peripheral Vortex Veins**
	1. Drains Choroid, Ciliary Body, Uveal tract
2. **Central Retinal Vein**
	1. Drains Inner Retina
3. **Superior and Inferior Ophthalmic Veins**
	1. **Superior**
		1. Drains Upper Globe, EOM, lacrimal Gland, Upper Eyelid, Ethmoid Sinus
	2. **Inferior**
		1. Drains Lower Globe, Lower Lid, Inferior Rectus and Inferior Oblique.
4. **Main Ophthalmic Vein**
5. **Anterior Ciliary Veins**
	1. Drains Anterior segment, Conjunctiva, Episcleral
6. **Cavernous Sinus**
