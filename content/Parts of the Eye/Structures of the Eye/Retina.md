# Layers
--- 
Listed from most inner (Anterior) to most outer (Posterior)
1. Inner limiting membrane (ILM)
2. Nerve fiber layer (NFL)
	1. Superficial retinal vasculature from [Central Retinal Artery](obsidian://open?vault=Diseases%20and%20Associated%20Symptoms&file=Optometry%20Notes%2FParts%20of%20the%20Eye%2FSurrounding%20Anatomy%2FSupply%2FVascular%20Supply)
3. Ganglion cell layer (GCL)
4. Inner plexiform layer (IPL)
5. Inner nuclear layer (INL)
	1. Deep retinal vasculature from [Central Retinal Artery](obsidian://open?vault=Diseases%20and%20Associated%20Symptoms&file=Optometry%20Notes%2FParts%20of%20the%20Eye%2FSurrounding%20Anatomy%2FSupply%2FVascular%20Supply)
6. Outer plexiform layer (OPL)
7. Outer nuclear Layer (ONL)
8. External limiting membrane (ELM)
9. Photoreceptor layer 
	1. Inner segments
	2. Outer segments
10. Retinal pigment epithelium
	1. Photoreceptor layer
