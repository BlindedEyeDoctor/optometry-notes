#Biomicrosopy 

A systematic slit‑lamp examination of the anterior eye using controlled illumination and magnification to assess ocular structures layer‑by‑layer. It allows you to evaluate clarity, contour, thickness, vascularity, and any pathological changes across the lids, conjunctiva, cornea, anterior chamber, iris, and lens.
