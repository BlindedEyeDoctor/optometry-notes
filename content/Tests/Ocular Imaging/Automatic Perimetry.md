# Automatic Perimetry
---
A computer build into a massive machine runs this test for you!

- In a 24-2 test, the 24 is essentially 24° degrees away from fixation it is testing for, the bigger the number, the wider the field. 
- The -2 means that the testing grid if offset by 3 degrees of the vertical and horizontal meridian.
Cool fact: it is at these meridians where we see a flicker or a subtle movement just at the corner of our eye. Its not really accurate and thus standard procedure has this offset by a couple degrees.
