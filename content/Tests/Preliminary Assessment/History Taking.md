# Structure - History Taking
---
History taking is most effective following the acronym LOFTSEA, which abbreviates for:
- L - Location/Laterality
	- "Where is the symptom?" 
		- ==Informs of visual field defect.==
	- "Which eye is affected?"
		- ==Differentiates between genetic and systemic components.==
- O - Onset
	- "When did it start?"
	- "Was it sudden or gradual?"
		- ==Implicates the severity of condition.==
- F - Frequency
	- "How often does it happen?"
	- "How long does it last?"
	- "Where does it happen"
- T - Type/Severity
	- What does the pain or symptom feel like?
	- Is it sharp or a dull throbbing?
- S - Self Medication
	- Has the patient taken any medication or tried any remedies?
- E - Effect on the patient/Daily Life
	- How does it impact everyday routine?
- A - Associated Factors/Symptoms
	- Are there any secondary symptoms, like nausea or blurred vision?
