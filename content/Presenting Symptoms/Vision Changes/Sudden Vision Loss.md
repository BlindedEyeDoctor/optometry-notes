# **Associated Diseases**

**Takeaway:** Sudden = **vascular**, **retinal**, or **optic nerve** emergency.

### 🔥 Retinal Artery Occlusions

- **CRAO** (profound, instantaneous central loss)
    
- **BRAO** (sectoral sudden loss)
    

### 🔥 Retinal Vein Occlusions

- **CRVO** (sudden blur → central loss if macular edema)
    
- **BRVO** (sudden patchy loss)
    

### 🔥 Retinal Structural Emergencies

- **Retinal Detachment**
    
- **Retinal Tear**
    
- **Vitreous Hemorrhage**
    

### 🔥 Optic Nerve Emergencies

- **AION** (altitudinal sudden loss)
    
- **Optic neuritis** (subacute but still sudden to patient)
    

### 🔥 Glaucoma / Pressure Spikes

- **Acute Angle‑Closure Glaucoma** Sudden blur + halos + pain + nausea.
    

### 🔥 Other

- **Macular hole (acute onset)**
    
- **Severe trauma**
