# **Associated Diseases**

**Mechanisms:** macula, fovea, optic nerve

### Macular

- AMD (dry/wet)
    
- Central Serous Chorioretinopathy (CSR)
    
- Macular hole
    
- Epiretinal membrane (ERM)
    
- Diabetic Macular Edema (DME)
    
- Macular ischemia (CRVO/BRVO)
    

### Vascular Occlusions

- **CRAO** (profound central loss)
    
- **CRVO** (macular edema → central loss)
    

### Optic Nerve

- Optic neuritis
    
- AION
    
- Toxic optic neuropathy
