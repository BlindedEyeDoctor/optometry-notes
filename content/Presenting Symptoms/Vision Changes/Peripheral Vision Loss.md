# **Associated Diseases**

**Mechanisms:** retina periphery, optic nerve, glaucoma

### Glaucoma

- Open‑angle glaucoma
    
- Angle‑closure glaucoma (late)
    

### Retinal

- Retinal detachment
    
- Retinitis pigmentosa
    
- Severe peripheral ischemia (BRVO/CRVO)
    

### Neurological

- Stroke affecting visual pathways (rare peripheral‑only loss)
