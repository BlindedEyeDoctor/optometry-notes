# **Associated Diseases**
**Mechanisms:** eye movements, palsies, paralysis, phorias

### Binocular Diplopia (EOM / Nerve)

- CN III palsy
    
- CN IV palsy
    
- CN VI palsy
    
- Thyroid Eye Disease (TED)
    
- Myasthenia Gravis
    
- Orbital mass / IOI
    
- Trauma
    

### Monocular Diplopia (optical)

- Keratoconus
    
- Cataract
    
- Lens subluxation
    
- Corneal irregularity
    
- Tear film instability
    

### Phorias / Decompensation

- Convergence insufficiency
    
- Divergence insufficiency
    
- Vertical phoria decompensation
