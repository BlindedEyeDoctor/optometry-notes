# Description
---
Reduced visual acuity.
# Relevance
---
1. Refraction
2. Tear Film
3. Cornea
4. Lens
5. Vitreous
### Refractive
- Myopia / Hyperopia / Astigmatism
- Presbyopia
- Keratoconus
### Tear Film
- Dry Eye Disease
- Meibomian Gland Dysfunction
- Blepharitis
# Corneal
- Abrasion
- Keratitis (bacterial, viral, fungal, Acanthamoeba)
- EBMD
- RCE
- Corneal edema (Fuchs, CL overwear)
### Lens
- Cataracts (NS, cortical, PSC)
- Lens dislocation / subluxation
### Vitreous
- PVD
- Vitreous hemorrhage
- Asteroid hyalosis
- PVD‑related traction
