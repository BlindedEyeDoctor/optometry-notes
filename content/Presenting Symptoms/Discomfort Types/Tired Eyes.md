# Description
This is your “tired eyes” symptom — often described as **fatigue, heaviness, or strain**, especially later in the day.
# Diagnostic Testing 
- Presentation
	- PX reports eyes feeling tired/strained
- Recommended Tests
	- Biomicroscopy
		- Assess cornea, conjunctiva, eyelid, eyelid margin perform eyelid inversion
		- See Below for Associated Symptoms
	- Refraction
		- Assess prescription
		- See Below for Associated Symptoms
# Differential Diagnosis
- **Dry Eye Disease** Tear film evaporates → end‑of‑day fatigue.
- **Meibomian Gland Dysfunction (MGD)** Poor lipid layer → tired eyes after prolonged use.
- **Uncorrected Refractive Error** Especially hyperopia or astigmatism → fatigue with near work.
- **Presbyopia** Near fatigue, especially late in the day.
- **Convergence Insufficiency** Tired eyes with reading, diplopia, headaches.
- **Accommodative Insufficiency / Spasm** Fatigue, blur, strain.
- **Computer Vision Syndrome** Digital strain → tired eyes + dryness.
- **Blepharitis** Lid inflammation → heaviness.
# Associated Symptoms
- End‑of‑day blur
- Eye strain
- Difficulty focusing
- Headaches (frontal)
- Burning or dryness
- Reduced blink rate (screens)
- Relief after resting eyes
