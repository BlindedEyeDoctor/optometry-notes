# Description
Grittiness = **ocular surface disease**.
# Diagnostic Testing 
- Presentation
	- PX reports grittiness or dryness
- Recommended Tests
	- Biomicroscopy
		- Assess cornea, conjunctiva, eyelid, eyelid margin perform eyelid inversion
		- See Below for Associated Symptoms
	- Dry Eye Testing
		- Assess tear film integrity
		- See Below for Associated Symptoms
# Differential Diagnosis
- **Dry Eye Disease (aqueous or evaporative)**
- **Meibomian Gland Dysfunction (MGD)**
- **Blepharitis**
- **Conjunctivitis (viral/bacterial)**
- **Epithelial Basement Membrane Dystrophy (EBMD)**
- **Recurrent Corneal Erosion (RCE)**
- **Contact Lens Overwear**
- **Giant Papillary Conjuntivitis** 
# Associated Symptoms
- Fluctuating blur
- Burning
- Foreign body sensation
- Worse with wind/aircon
- Worse at end of day
- Photophobia (if corneal involvement)
