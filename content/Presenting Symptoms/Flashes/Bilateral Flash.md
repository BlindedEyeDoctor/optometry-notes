# Description
Both eyes = **neurological** or **systemic**.
# Diagnostic Testing 
- Presentation
	- PX reports flashes in both eyes
- Recommended Tests
	- Visual Acuity
		- Assess vision quality
	- Pupil Testing
		- Look for Neurological Damage
	- Fundoscopy| Dilated Fundus Exam
		- Check peripheral retina for degeneration/tears/detachment
		- Evaluate optic nerve head size for neuritis
		- Track vascular changes for hemorrages
	- Visual Fields
		- Look for loss of visual fields, indicative of Retinal Detachment
	- Motility
		- Look for Neurological Damage
	- Optical Coherence Tomography|OCT
		- Look for Retinal Detachment
	- Blood Work |  BP Measurement
		- Hyper/Hypotension evaluation
	- Tonometry | IOP Assessment
		- Look for high and low IOP
# Differential Diagnosis
- **Migraine aura** Scintillating zigzags, shimmering lights.
- **Occipital lobe ischemia / TIA**
- **Papilledema** Transient obscurations + bilateral flashes.
- **Hypotension / syncope episodes**
- **Digital eye strain (non‑pathologic)** Brief bilateral flickers.
# Key Clues
- Symmetrical
- Often accompanied by headache or systemic symptoms
- Usually **not** retinal
