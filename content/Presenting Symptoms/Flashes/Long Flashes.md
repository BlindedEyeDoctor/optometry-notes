# Description
Long‑lasting flashes = **neurological** or **severe traction**.
# Diagnostic Testing 
- Presentation
	- PX reports flashes progressing longer than usaul
- Recommended Tests
	- Visual Acuity
		- Assess vision quality
	- Fundoscopy| Dilated Fundus Exam
		- Look for retinal traction, retinal tears 
	- Pupil Testing
		- Look for Neurological Damage
	- Optical Coherence Tomography | OCT
		- Look for Retinal Detachment
	- Visual Fields
		- Look for loss of visual fields, indicative of Retinal Detachment
	- Motility
		- Look for Neurological Damage
	-  Tonometry | IOP Assessment
		- Look for high and low IOP
# Differential Diagnosis
- **Migraine aura** Lasts 10–30 minutes.
- **Occipital lobe ischemia / TIA**
- **Papilledema (transient obscurations)**
- **Severe vitreoretinal traction** Persistent photopsia.
- **Retinal detachment (progressing)** Long flashes + curtain.
# Key Clues
- Duration > 1–2 seconds
- Often accompanied by visual distortion
- Persistent flashes = traction
- Long shimmering = migraine aura
