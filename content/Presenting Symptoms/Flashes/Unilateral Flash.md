# Description
One‑sided flashes = **traction** or **retinal pathology** in that eye.
# Diagnostic Testing 
- Presentation
	- PX reports flashes in one eye
- Recommended Tests
	- Visual Acuity
		- Assess vision quality
	- Fundoscopy| Dilated Fundus Exam
		- Look for retinal traction, retinal tears 
	- Pupil Testing
		- Look for Neurological Damage
	- Optical Coherence Tomography | OCT
		- Look for Retinal Detachment
	- Visual Fields
		- Look for loss of visual fields, indicative of Retinal Detachment
	- Motility
		- Look for Neurological Damage
	- Tonometry | IOP Assessment
		- Look for high and low IOP
# Differential Diagnosis
- **Posterior Vitreous Detachment (PVD)**
- **Retinal Tear**
- **Retinal Detachment**
- **Vitreous Traction**
- **Migraine aura (rare unilateral)**
- **Ocular trauma**
- **Lattice degeneration with traction**
# Key Clues
- Sudden onset
- Often accompanied by floaters
- Curtain or shadow = detachment
- Unilateral photopsia = retinal traction until proven otherwise
