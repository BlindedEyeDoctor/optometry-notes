# Associated Diseases
Brief, momentary flashes = **traction** or **migraine aura**.
# Diagnostic Testing 
- Presentation
	- PX reports flashes appearing and disappearing more punctually
- Recommended Tests
	- Visual Acuity
		- Assess vision quality
	- Fundoscopy| Dilated Fundus Exam
		- Look for retinal traction, retinal tears 
	- Pupil Testing
		- Look for Neurological Damage
	- Optical Coherence Tomography | OCT
		- Look for Retinal Detachment
	- Visual Fields
		- Look for loss of visual fields, indicative of Retinal Detachment
	- Motility
		- Look for Neurological Damage
	-  Tonometry | IOP Assessment
		- Look for high and low IOP
# Differential Diagnosis
- **PVD traction**
- **Retinal tear (early)**
- **Retinal detachment (early)**
- **Migraine aura**
- **Occipital lobe irritation**
- **Digital strain / photostress**
# Key Clues
- Millisecond‑duration
- Often triggered by eye movement
- “Lightning streaks” = retinal traction
- “Shimmering zigzags” = migraine aura
