# Description 
Bright red, superficial vessels; most intense in the **fornices**, fades toward the limbus. **Vessels:** Move with a cotton bud; blanch with phenylephrine.
# Diagnostic Testing 
- Presentation
	- History Taking
		- Might be reported by patient
- Recommended Tests
	- Visual Acuity
		- Assess vision quality
	- Biomicroscopy
		- Assess Lids, Conjunctiva, Cornea, Sclera, Iris, eyelid inversion. 
	- Pupil Testing
		- Look for Neurological Damage
	- Motility
		- Look for Neurological Damage
	- Tonometry | IOP Assessment 
		- Look for Glaucoma 
# Associated Diseases
- Bacterial Conjunctivitis
- Allergic Conjunctivitis
- Viral Conjunctivitis
- Dry eye
- Irritant exposure

# Key clues:
- **Itching → allergy**
- **Mucopurulent discharge → bacterial**
- **Watery + preauricular node → viral**
- **Burning/grittiness → dry eye**
