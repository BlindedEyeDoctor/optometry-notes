## **Pattern:** 
Localized bright red patch; usually **sectoral**, not diffuse. **Vessels:** Blanch with phenylephrine; tender but mild discomfort.

## **Common causes:**
- Idiopathic
- Autoimmune associations (RA, IBD)
- [[Scratching]]

## **Key clues:**
- **Mild discomfort
- No photophobia**
- **Blanching with Phenylephrine helps differentiate from scleritis**
