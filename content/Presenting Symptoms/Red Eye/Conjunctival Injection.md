Not true “swelling,” but clinically grouped with swelling patterns because the conjunctiva appears **engorged**.

**Associated diseases:**

- Viral conjunctivitis
    
- Bacterial conjunctivitis
    
- Allergic conjunctivitis
    
- Dry eye
    
- Irritant exposure
    

**Associated symptoms:**

- Redness
    
- Discharge
    
- Burning
    
- Grittiness
