**Pattern:** Well‑demarcated, bright red patch under conjunctiva. **Vessels:** None — it’s a sheet of blood.

**Common causes:**

- Trauma
    
- Coughing/sneezing
    
- Hypertension
    
- Blood thinners
    

**Key clues:**

- **No pain, no discharge, normal vision**
    
- **Looks dramatic but benign**
