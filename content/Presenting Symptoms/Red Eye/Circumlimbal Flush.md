# Description
**Violaceous ring** around the limbus (perilimbal redness). **Vessels:** Deep, non‑blanching.
# Diagnostic Testing 
- Presentation
	- History Taking
		- Might be reported by patient
- Recommended Tests
	- Visual Acuity
		- Assess vision quality
	- Biomicroscopy
		- Assess Lids, Conjunctiva, Cornea, Sclera, Iris, eyelid inversion. 
	- Pupil Testing
		- Look for Neurological Damage
	- Motility
		- Look for Neurological Damage
	- Tonometry | IOP Assessment 
		- Look for Glaucoma 
# Associated Diseases
- Anterior Uveitis
- Keratitis
- Posterior Uveitis
- **Acute angle‑closure glaucoma**
