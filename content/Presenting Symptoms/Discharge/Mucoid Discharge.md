# Description
---
**Stringy, white, rope‑like**, or gelatinous discharge. Mucin‑rich discharge from **allergy or chronic irritation**.
# Associated Symptoms
- Itching
- Grittiness
- Watering
- Papillae (allergy)
- Burning (dry eye)
- CL intolerance (GPC)
# Diagnostic Testing 
---
# Presentation
---
- PX reports crusting or more sleep in eyes
- PX reports eyes glued together
# Recommended Tests
---
-  Biomicroscopy
	- Assess cornea, conjunctiva, eyelid, eyelid margin perform eyelid inversion
# Differential Diagnosis
---
- **Allergic Conjunctivitis** 
	- Classic **stringy white** discharge + itching.
- **Dry Eye Disease** 
	- Thin white/filmy discharge from tear film instability.
- **Blepharitis / Meibomian Gland Dysfunction (MGD)** 
	- Foamy or bubbly discharge; crusting.
- **Contact Lens Papillary Conjunctivitis (GPC)** 
	- Mucous strands + CL intolerance. (Supported by CL‑related mucus plaques)
- **Viral Conjunctivitis (sometimes)** 
	- Watery discharge with occasional white mucus component.
