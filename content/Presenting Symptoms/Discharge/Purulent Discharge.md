# Description
---
Thick **Yellow or Green**, pus‑like discharge. Neutrophil‑rich exudate from a **Bacterial Infection**.
# Presentation
---
PX reports crusting or more sleep in eyes
# Associated Symptoms
---
- Lids stuck shut
- Red eye
- Pain or soreness
- Rapid re‑accumulation after wiping
- Photophobia (if corneal involvement)
# Recommended Tests
---
-  Biomicroscopy
	- Assess cornea, conjunctiva, eyelid, eyelid margin perform eyelid inversion
# Differential Diagnosis
---
- **Bacterial Conjunctivitis** 
	- Thick yellow/green discharge; lids stuck shut on waking.
- **Severe Bacterial Infection (e.g., Gonococcal)** 
	- Rapidly reforming green discharge; high risk in CL wearers.
- **Stye / Hordeolum** 
	- Localised pus + lid tenderness.
- **Dacryocystitis** 
	- Yellow discharge expressed from punctum; painful medial swelling.
- **Corneal Ulcer / Keratitis (bacterial)** 
	- Thick discharge + pain + photophobia.
