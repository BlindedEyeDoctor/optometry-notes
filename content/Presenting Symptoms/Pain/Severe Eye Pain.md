Pain that stops normal activity; patient often distressed.

### **Associated diseases**

- **Keratitis (bacterial, fungal, Acanthamoeba)** Severe pain out of proportion to signs.
    
- **Anterior Uveitis (moderate–severe)** Consensual photophobia + deep ache.
    
- **Acute Angle‑Closure Glaucoma** Severe pain + nausea + halos + mid‑dilated pupil.
    
- **Scleritis (early)** Deep, intense pain.
    

### **Clinical pearl:**

Severe pain + reduced vision → **urgent referral**.
