# Description
Usually **surface-level**, non‑vision‑threatening.
# Diagnostic Testing 
- Presentation
	- History Taking
- Recommended Tests
	- Visual Acuity
		- Assess vision quality
	- Biomicroscopy
		- Assess Lids, Conjunctiva, Cornea, Sclera, Iris, eyelid inversion. 
	- Pupil Testing
		- Look for Neurological Damage
	- Motility
		- Look for Neurological Damage
	- Tonometry | IOP Assessment 
		- Look for high and low IOP
	- Dry Eye Testing
		- Look for Dry Eye
# Associated diseases
- Tear Film Diseases Burning, grittiness, worse with wind/aircon.
- **Allergic Conjunctivitis** Itching + mild irritation.
- **Viral Conjunctivitis** Mild ache + watery discharge.
- **Bacterial Conjunctivitis** Mild discomfort + mucopurulent discharge.
- **Episcleritis** Sectoral redness, mild tenderness.
- **Blepharitis** Lid margin irritation, crusting.
# Clinical pearl:
If pain is mild and vision is normal → usually **conjunctiva or lids**.
