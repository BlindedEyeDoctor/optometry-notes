This is the “I can’t open my eye, I’m dying” level.

### **Associated diseases**

- **Acanthamoeba Keratitis** _Pain massively out of proportion to clinical signs._
    
- **Severe Bacterial Keratitis** Ulcer + hypopyon + intense pain.
    
- **Corneal Laceration / Penetrating Injury** Extreme pain + tearing + photophobia.
    
- **Acute Angle‑Closure Glaucoma (full attack)** Crushing pain + vomiting + cloudy cornea.
    
- **Severe Uveitis (fibrin, hypopyon)** Deep ache + photophobia + vision loss.
    

### **Clinical pearl:**

Extreme pain → **cornea or intraocular pressure** until proven otherwise.
