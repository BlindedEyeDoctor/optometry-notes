This is a _distinct_ pain quality — patients describe it as **boring into the bone**, radiating to the **temple, jaw, or brow**.

### **Associated diseases**

- **Scleritis (anterior or posterior)** Classic deep, boring pain; worse at night; pain on eye movement.
    
- **Posterior Uveitis (rare)** Deep ache but usually less intense than scleritis.
    
- **Optic Neuritis (retrobulbar)** Pain with eye movement + decreased colour vision.
    
- **Orbital Cellulitis** Deep pain + fever + pain on eye movement.
    
- **Sinus‑related orbital inflammation** Deep pressure pain behind the eye.
    

### **Clinical pearl:**

Deep boring pain → **scleritis** until proven otherwise.
