# Description
Pain that makes the patient uncomfortable but still functional.
# Diagnostic Testing 
- Presentation
	- History Taking
- Recommended Tests
	- Visual Acuity
		- Assess vision quality
	- Biomicroscopy
		- Assess Lids, Conjunctiva, Cornea, Sclera, Iris, eyelid inversion. 
	- Fundoscopy| Slit Lamp Funduscopy
		- Assessing 
	- Pupil Testing
		- Look for Neurological Damage
	- Motility
		- Look for Neurological Damage
	- Tonometry | IOP Assessment 
		- Look for high and low IOP
	- Dry Eye Testing
		- Look for Dry Eye
# Associated diseases
- **Keratitis (early)** Pain + photophobia + epithelial defect.
- **Anterior Uveitis (early)** Achy pain + photophobia + circumlimbal flush.
- **Corneal Foreign Body** Sharp pain, tearing, photophobia.
- **Corneal Abrasion** Moderate pain, worse with blinking.
- **Chemical Irritation** Pain after exposure to irritants.
# Clinical pearl:
Moderate pain + photophobia → think **cornea or uvea**.
