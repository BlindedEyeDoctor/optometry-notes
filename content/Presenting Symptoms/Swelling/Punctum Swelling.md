Inflammation of the **punctum** (the tear drainage opening).

### **Associated Diseases**

- **Punctal Stenosis**
    
- **Punctal Inflammation (Punctitis)**
    
- **Canaliculitis** Often caused by _Actinomyces_.
    

### **Associated Symptoms**

- Tender punctum
    
- Redness at medial canthus
    
- Mucous discharge
    
- “Pouting punctum”
    
- Epiphora (watering)
