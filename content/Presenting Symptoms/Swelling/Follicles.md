Round, **translucent**, avascular swellings — lymphoid tissue.

### **Associated Diseases**

- **Viral Conjunctivitis (Adenovirus)**
    
- **Chlamydial Conjunctivitis**
    
- **Toxic Conjunctivitis** (medications)
    
- **Allergic (sometimes)**
    

### **Associated Symptoms**

- Watering
    
- Preauricular lymph node
    
- Photophobia (viral)
    
- Stringy discharge (chlamydia)
    
- Redness
    
- Grittiness
