**What it is:** Balloon‑like swelling of the conjunctiva (gelatinous, boggy).

**Associated diseases:**

- Allergic conjunctivitis
    
- Severe bacterial conjunctivitis
    
- Orbital cellulitis
    
- Thyroid eye disease
    
- Angioedema
    
- Chemical/irritant exposure
    

**Associated symptoms:**

- Boggy conjunctiva
    
- Redness
    
- Tearing
    
- Possible proptosis (if orbital cause)
